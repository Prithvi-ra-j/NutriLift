# PDF Export Feature Guide

## Overview
The PDF Export feature allows you to generate comprehensive progress reports that include all your nutrition logs, workout data, body stats, recovery metrics, and personal records.

## How to Access

1. Open the Apex app
2. Navigate to the **More** tab (bottom navigation)
3. Select the **Settings** section
4. Tap on **"Export Progress PDF"**

## Export Options

When you tap the export button, you'll see three options:

### 1. Last 30 Days
- **Best for**: Monthly progress reviews
- **Includes**: All data from the past 30 days
- **Use case**: Regular monthly check-ins, sharing with coach/trainer

### 2. Current Month
- **Best for**: Month-to-date tracking
- **Includes**: All data from the 1st of current month to today
- **Use case**: Mid-month reviews, monthly goal tracking

### 3. All Data
- **Best for**: Complete training history
- **Includes**: Every single log since you started using Apex
- **Use case**: Annual reviews, comprehensive progress analysis, switching trainers

## What's Included in the PDF

### 📊 Summary Section
- Average daily protein intake
- Average daily calories
- Days protein target was hit
- Total workout volume
- Number of training sessions

### 🍽️ Nutrition Logs
**Daily Summary Table**:
- Date
- Total calories
- Protein, carbs, fat breakdown
- Target hit/miss status

**Detailed Food Logs**:
- Organized by date and meal
- Food name and quantity
- Complete macro breakdown per item

### 💪 Workout Logs
**Session Overview**:
- Date and day type (Push A, Pull A, etc.)
- Duration and total volume
- Session RPE

**Exercise Details**:
- Exercise name, muscle group, equipment
- All sets with weight, reps, RPE
- PR badges on record-breaking sets
- Warmup vs working set indicators

### 📏 Body Stats
- Date of measurement
- Weight (kg)
- Body fat percentage
- Skeletal muscle mass
- BMI
- InBody score (if available)

### 😴 Recovery Logs
- Sleep duration and quality
- Energy level (1-5)
- Muscle soreness (1-5)
- Stress level (1-5)
- HRV (Heart Rate Variability)
- Resting heart rate

### 🏆 Personal Records
- Exercise name
- Best weight lifted
- Reps at best weight
- Estimated 1RM
- Best single-set volume
- Date achieved

## Sharing the PDF

After generation, the PDF will automatically open a share dialog where you can:

- 📧 **Email** to yourself or your coach
- 💬 **Message** via WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.
- ☁️ **Save to cloud** (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox)
- 📱 **AirDrop** to other devices (iOS)
- 💾 **Save locally** to your device

### File Location
- **iOS**: Saved to Documents folder
- **Android**: Saved to Downloads folder

### File Naming
Files are automatically named with the date:
```
apex-progress-2026-05-10.pdf
```

## Tips for Best Results

### Before Exporting
1. **Ensure data is complete**: Log all meals and workouts before exporting
2. **Update body stats**: Add recent weight or InBody measurements
3. **Log recovery**: Fill in sleep and recovery data for complete picture

### After Exporting
1. **Review the PDF**: Check that all data looks correct
2. **Add context**: When sharing, add a message explaining what the recipient should focus on
3. **Keep copies**: Save PDFs monthly to track long-term trends

### For Large Exports
- **All Data** exports may take 5-10 seconds to generate if you have months of data
- Be patient and don't close the app during generation
- Ensure you have sufficient storage space on your device

## Troubleshooting

### PDF Generation Failed
- **Check storage space**: Ensure you have at least 50MB free
- **Try smaller range**: If "All Data" fails, try "Last 30 Days"
- **Restart app**: Close and reopen Apex, then try again

### Share Dialog Not Opening
- **Check permissions**: Ensure Apex has file access permissions
- **Manual access**: Navigate to Documents (iOS) or Downloads (Android) to find the PDF

### Missing Data in PDF
- **Verify data exists**: Check the relevant tabs (Nutrition, Workout) to confirm data is logged
- **Check date range**: Ensure the export period includes the dates you're looking for

### PDF Looks Incomplete
- **Large datasets**: Very large exports may be truncated; try smaller date ranges
- **Specific sections**: If only certain sections are missing, that data type may not be logged

## Privacy & Security
